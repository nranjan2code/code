## Traps learned this session

- Two sessions share this checkout. `git add -A` commits the other
  session's unfinished work: stage your own paths only. Its half-finished
  edits can break the build mid-session: wait for its commit rather than
  gating over them, and stop your own gate (`pkill -f scripts/gate.sh`)
  if its files change under it.
- The other session added invariant 43 (provenance: the owner's data and
  someone else's words together put every exit to a person) and
  `Finding::Declined` (a tool the person declined leaves the turn). A live
  turn that fetches a page after reading private data now asks first.
- `/runs/{id}/decisions` returns nothing while the run is open on this
  build; poll the run's status, then read its decisions.
- A `/until-done` turn is released by a steering message that is exactly
  `done` (`POST /sessions/{id}/steering {"text":"done"}`).
- `scripts/measure_tool_form.py replay` records outcomes, not the
  response: to see how a no-call response ended, post the captured body
  to `/api/chat` with `stream: false` and read `done_reason`,
  `eval_count`, `tool_calls` and the thinking's tail.
- `/library/{id}/versions/{v}` returns a version's bytes; that is how to
  put an earlier version in the folder for a Compare check.
- `tempfile` is a dev dependency of vak-server only; temporary files a
  route needs go under `vak_config::paths::runtime_dir()`.
