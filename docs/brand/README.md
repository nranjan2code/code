# Vakyartha brand

The public brand is **Vakyartha**, including on the official site at
https://vakyartha.com. The **Vakyartha Songbird** is the identity mark across
the app, desktop, web, admin, cards, print, and public communication. Keep
`vak` for CLI commands, crate names, identifiers, paths and wire formats; the
small app icon uses the bird symbol without shortening the public name.

## Meaning

Vākyārtha names the meaning carried by a sentence. The Songbird gives that
idea a recognisable character: an attentive companion that receives an idea,
understands it, and helps carry it into useful work. The artwork imagines a
crested songbird rather than depicting one exact species.

- Its attentive gaze signals listening before action.
- The three-feather crest carries the rhythm of a voice.
- The open V in the wing connects the bird to **Vakyartha**.
- The saffron throat marks expression and response.
- The indigo silhouette brings calm, confident weight.

This is an editorial story for the design, not a claim that Vakyartha is
omniscient or infallible. Product copy should say what the agent can actually
do, and how the person can inspect and guide it.

## Source artwork

`mark/vakyartha-songbird.svg` is the clean vector master. It is flat,
geometric SVG built from separate silhouette, negative-space and throat
shapes. Its three anchors are the swept crest, the V-shaped wing, and the
saffron throat.

The public horizontal identity pairs this symbol with the complete,
custom-outlined **Vakyartha** wordmark. `Manrope` Semibold at weight 600
provides a clear humanist voice in the generated lockup;
`fonts/OFL-Manrope.txt` carries its SIL Open Font License 1.1. The exported
wordmark consists of paths, so it renders the same at screen and print sizes
without a font dependency.

The prior interwoven V, from the approved 2026-09-25 identity, is preserved
as historical artwork at `history/2026-09-25-woven-v.png`. It is no longer
the current logo.

## Ready-to-use artwork

The artwork in `exports/` is generated by `scripts/brand/generate.mjs`.
Never edit generated files by hand; change the SVG master or the generator,
then regenerate every destination together.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `vakyartha-songbird-colour.svg` | Master symbol on a light ground |
| `vakyartha-songbird-reverse.svg` | White symbol on a dark ground |
| `vakyartha-songbird-black.svg`, `vakyartha-songbird-white.svg` | One-ink production, engraving, stamps |
| `vakyartha-lockup-colour.svg`, `vakyartha-lockup-reverse.svg` | Complete name and bird for web, letters and cards |
| `vakyartha-lockup-black.svg`, `vakyartha-lockup-white.svg` | One-ink complete identity |
| `vakyartha-wordmark-*.svg` | Full-name wordmark |
| `vakyartha-card-front.svg`, `vakyartha-card-back.svg` | Editable 3.5 × 2 in business-card artwork with 0.125 in bleed and crop marks; replace the name, role and email fields before printing |
| `vakyartha-songbird-*.png`, `vakyartha-lockup-*.png` | High-resolution raster exports |
| `vakyartha-app-icon.svg`, `vakyartha-app-icon.png` | Complete indigo-tile app icon |

Generated app bundles also contain the current icon, light/dark wordmark paths,
favicons, notification art, Dock artwork, browser install icons, touch icons,
Android and iOS icons, and platform-specific tray icons. Their source vector
art is in `crates/vak-client-ui/public/assets/brand/`.

## Colour and type

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Indigo ink | `#101D3D` | Bird, icon tile, wordmark |
| Saffron | `#F5A400` | Bird's throat |
| Warm paper | `#F6F5F1` | Face, reverse artwork, light print ground |

Indigo and paper ground the mark. Saffron stays focused on the bird's throat
and on the product's established live states. Use solid fills. Keep the
complete, centred symbol in app tiles. Use the standalone bird for a square,
small or co-branded space; use the horizontal lockup where the full public
name must be clear.

Product body text uses the design system's interface type. The custom
wordmark is outlined artwork; marketing headings may use the existing
Newsreader family.

## Voice and language

Use **Vakyartha** in public titles, product writing, support and narration.
The internal `vak` command and namespaces keep their established spellings.
Avoid abbreviations such as Vak, getvak and “Vakyartha AI” as public names.

Campaign line: **Ask. Then go live your day.**

Product description: **An attentive agent that helps you carry your ideas
through into useful work.**

Tagline: *An agent you can inspect, constrain, and extend.*

The Vakyartha voice is calm, confident and conversational. The two-note
songbird chirp at `docs/video/spot-2026/audio/sfx-chirp.mp3` is available for
video end cards and the bird's arrival.

## Production and provenance

Run on macOS (Node 20.9+; `iconutil` is supplied by macOS):

```sh
npm ci --prefix scripts/brand
npm --prefix scripts/brand run generate
npm --prefix scripts/brand run check
```

The isolated asset generator pins `sharp` 0.35.4 for deterministic platform
renders and `fontkit` 2.0.4 to turn the licensed wordmark into stable vector
paths. Rebuild the client, admin and public-site bundles after asset or brand
copy changes; generated bundles are checked into the repository.

Respect platform corner masks. Design the source symbol with open negative
space; preserve clear space when pairing the full name. Check the bird at
16, 24, 32 and 64px and in black-only print before adoption on a new surface.
Use the provided vector for commercial print; consult the printer for ink
matching on a specific stock.

Historical reviews and visual studies are under
`docs/brand/explorations/` and `docs/brand/history/`.

## Brand principles

- The public name is Vakyartha.
- Show the bird's open V, crest and saffron throat without gradients, glow,
  bevels, photorealism or added tile shapes.
- Keep each composition to one clear accent.
- Present Vakyartha as a universal everyday agent, not a developer-only tool.
- Respect the official domain: https://vakyartha.com.
- Never invent benchmarks, customer names or testimonials.
