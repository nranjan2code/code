# Vakyartha — identity exploration

Status: **concept proposals, 2026-09-26; not an approved replacement.**

Public name: **Vakyartha**. Official domain: **vakyartha.com**.
The website redesign is a later task. Existing production assets are unchanged.

## Brief and research

Vakyartha is a general-purpose agent: people express an intention, the agent
carries work through, and people can inspect and constrain what happens.
The identity should convey understanding, capable action and calm confidence
across everyday work, writing, research, operations and engineering.

- The implemented product model is [64-agent-owned-platform.md](../../../design/64-agent-owned-platform.md):
  choose an Agent, talk, receive a useful result. The [brand record](../../README.md)
  supplies the existing indigo/saffron palette and songbird.
- Vākyārtha refers to sentence meaning. “Meaning into action” is our design
  interpretation, not a literal translation. See the [IGNOU language study text](https://www.egyankosh.ac.in/bitstream/123456789/111296/1/Block-4.pdf)
  (indexed excerpt consulted; direct PDF retrieval failed).
- [Apple's app-icon guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons)
  recommends a distinctive core idea, few shapes, centered content and consistency
  across platforms. This motivates substantial shapes and open counters.
- [Cursor's guidelines](https://cursor.com/brand) distinguish lockups, wordmark,
  symbol, app icons and avatars: a useful model for coordinated assets.
- [OpenAI's guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/) describe its Blossom and
  wordmark. Our creative choice is to explore other silhouettes and avoid radial
  woven AI emblems. References inform differentiation, not imitation.

This is desk research and art direction, not audience testing or trademark
clearance. The official website could not be retrieved by the web tool and
was not used as evidence for product claims.

## Directions

- [03 — Intent Ligature](03-intent-ligature.png)

| Direction | Idea and personality | Main refinement question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Meaning Fold | Separate planes resolving into a purposeful form; architectural, precise, confident | Can it stand apart from other V and ribbon marks? |
| 02 Listening Bird | Attentive songbird distilled into a symbol; approachable and capable | Can the crest, face and wing survive genuinely small sizes? |
| 03 Intent Ligature | Original Latin v/a glyph; language, authorship, intention | Does it feel distinctive without reading as an unrelated letter? |

The boards show app icons, monochrome studies, web lockups and business-card
applications. These are raster concept previews, not production proofs.
The generated colour studies retain some tonal shading despite the flat-colour
brief; replace this with deliberate solid fills in final vector development.
Meaning Fold is the leading direction for a premium masterbrand; Listening Bird
is the stronger alternative if a recognisable companion should lead the brand.

## Production requirements for the selected direction

Create editable vector artwork with one geometry source and optically tuned
lettering. Define horizontal and stacked lockups, standalone symbol, reverse
and single-ink versions, and the full-name wordmark. Keep detail in the shape
and typography rather than adding decorative complexity.

Test the symbol at 16, 24, 32 and 64 px and at 6, 10 and 20 mm in print.
Determine minimum size from these tests. Verify relevant platform masks,
light/dark backgrounds, ordinary black ink, colour print, embossing and foil.
Proof saffron with the intended printer; screen RGB does not specify a print
match. Review visual similarity before final adoption.
