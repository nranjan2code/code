From a request to a result you can stand behind

Vakyartha does not treat the first plausible renderer as the product. It reads what you are trying to accomplish, admits a bounded engagement, records what happened, evaluates the result, and then chooses a presentation that fits the evidence and the surface.

The outcome loop

The loop is shared by the CLI, desktop app, browser client and channel gateways. A surface can change the view; it cannot change the contract.

1 · UnderstandTurn the request into an explicit objective, scope, constraints and success criteria. Uncertainty narrows the engagement instead of silently granting more.
2 · AdmitFreeze the allowed capability slice, route ladder, budget and approval floor before work begins. Intent can subtract authority; it never creates it.
3 · ExecutePlan, tools, model calls, approvals and side runs use the same broker and permission decision. Every model-visible input and effect is logged.
4 · EvaluateThe runtime checks criteria against receipts and observations. The model may propose a criterion, but it cannot mark its own work as proven.
5 · PresentThe result carries status, evidence strength, requirement links and goal state into the shared output timeline. Renderers select a view from that semantic contract.

A live run is collaborative

A new message is not automatically a distraction, and it is not automatically permission. Vakyartha classifies it against the active goal and makes the impact visible before applying it.

Add a requirementAttach new work to the current goal, show the added scope and replan only within the admitted limits.
Correct the directionRecord a correction as a new goal update, preserve the earlier plan, and show what it superseded.
Pause, resume or stopUse explicit controls that preserve partial output and append the control decision to the timeline.
Reprioritise safelyMove priorities or request a hard human decision when the change affects authority, irreversible work or the frozen route.

The same timeline exposes the original request, plan changes, approvals, tool activity, receipts, interventions and final evaluation. “Why this rendering?” is useful; “what happened and is it done?” is the primary view.

Evidence before polish

A beautiful answer with the wrong confidence is a product failure. Vakyartha keeps evidence attached to the claim it supports and degrades honestly when the preferred presentation is unavailable.

Observed means the runtime ran the command or captured the state. Attested means an external receipt supports it. Asserted means the model said it. Those are different facts in the ledger and in the UI.

A result can be complete, partial, blocked, failed or still in progress. The renderer does not upgrade that status, hide missing evidence or invent a richer widget. If a chart, diff view or specialised card is unavailable, the same semantic result falls back to exact Markdown.

For a coding task that may mean a diff and test matrix. For research it may mean takeaways with verified citations. For operations it may mean a timeline of actions and before/after receipts. For a general request it may simply mean a clear answer with the criteria and confidence beside it.

Extend the harness without teaching it to lie

Skills, hooks, MCP tools, renderers and providers are capabilities, not hidden exceptions. The registry can reconcile them while sessions live, and the workspace can propose a missing capability for review.

Workspace-scoped firstA generated skill, hook or renderer is confined to the workspace until it has been tested and deliberately promoted.
Human oversightInstallation, promotion, credential use and irreversible effects stay behind the existing permission and approval boundaries.
Live reconciliationAdditions appear at a safe turn boundary; revocations take effect immediately. A failed probe is visible state, never a phantom callable tool.
No hidden wideningAn extension can improve how Vakyartha works or presents an outcome, but it cannot raise a capability, budget or approval ceiling on its own.
