Vāk is expression
with consequences.

In Sanskrit, Vāk means speech, word, language, and voice—but its deeper reach is meaningful expression: thought made communicable, knowledge made transmissible, and words capable of shaping action.

वाक् / vāk
meaning made visible

What the name asks of the product

The name is not a claim that vak is spiritual, omniscient, or a learning-style quiz. It is a design standard: expression should retain meaning as it moves through a system.

01 / carry

Carry intention

A prompt is more than a string. vak understands the kind of work, its risk, and the proof it owes before choosing what may happen.

02 / preserve

Preserve meaning

Sessions are append-only. Memory is searchable and editable. Partial work survives cancellation. The path to a result stays visible.

03 / govern

Give expression boundaries

Language can become a command, a message, or a deployment. Permissions, approvals, and sandboxing decide when it may cross that boundary.

04 / answer

Return evidence

Every model dispatch leaves a receipt. A fluent answer is not the same thing as a verified outcome.
