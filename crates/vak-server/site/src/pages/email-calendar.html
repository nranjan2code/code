Mail and calendar stay in your control

Connect only the accounts you choose. Each routine names what it may read, how often it runs, where its result goes, and what it may propose. A send or calendar change waits for your review unless you give a narrow, revocable grant for that specific kind of work.

Examples show possible ways to work. What is available depends on your connected accounts, permissions, and setup.
