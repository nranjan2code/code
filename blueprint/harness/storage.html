    Code evidence and boundaries

      Retention fields start from the repository's shipped defaults, not this install's effective label. They are planning knobs. Actual removal belongs only to the lifecycle reconciler, under the effective label and its holds and guards. The temporary pool approximates scratch and environments; saved Library versions stay in permanent storage. Index overhead is an assumption on metadata, not a measured catalog size.

      Backups are full-payload equivalents across other locations; primary capacity excludes those copies. Real snapshot schedules, incremental reuse, erasure history and remote garbage collection differ. Initial data, filesystem allocation overhead, WAL peaks, checkpoints, attachments outside the modeled objects and unrelated workspace files are excluded. Recovery estimates are lower-bound work estimates, not promised timings.

      Full history repeated within one growing conversation makes cumulative input grow quadratically; resetting conversation length bounds that case. Disk growth follows new retained bytes, usually linearly at constant workload. Prompt caches affect billing, not the ledger retention contract.
